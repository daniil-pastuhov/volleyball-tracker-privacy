# Privacy Policy for Volleyball Camera Scoreboard

**Effective date: 18 November 2026**

## What this policy covers

This policy describes how the Android app **Volleyball Camera Scoreboard** (package `com.volleyball.tracker`, "the app") handles information. The app is published by **Daniil Pastukhou** ("we").

## Summary

- The app works entirely on your device. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- It uses the camera only to follow a moving ball on the screen. Camera pictures are analysed in memory and are never saved or transmitted.
- It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or advertising software.
- The only thing it stores is your match (team names, scores, point log and settings), in the app's private storage on your device.

## Camera

The app asks for the **Camera** permission. It is used for two things: to show the live camera picture on the screen, and to analyse that picture so that a moving ball can be followed and a point proposed when it lands.

- Each camera frame is reduced to a coarse brightness grid and compared with the previous ones, in the device's memory. The frame is then discarded.
- The app does not take photos, does not record video, does not record audio, and does not write any image to storage.
- The app does not identify people. People who are in front of the camera are visible in the live picture on the tablet's own screen, as with any camera viewfinder, but no image of them is kept.
- You can refuse or revoke the camera permission at any time in Android's settings. The scoreboard, manual scoring and the built-in simulator keep working without it.

## Information stored on your device

So that a match survives closing the app, the app saves the following in its private storage on the device:

- the team names you type in;
- the score, sets, timeouts and the point-by-point log (who scored, the reason, and the date and time of each point);
- your settings (match format, automatic awarding, serve check, detection sensitivity) and the court calibration.

This information never leaves the device through the app. The app turns off Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data, so it is not copied to your Google account or to a new device.

**Deleting it:** "Reset match" in the app clears the score and the log. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes everything the app has stored.

## Information we collect or share

None. We do not receive any information from the app: the app has no internet access and contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or social software. We therefore have nothing to sell, share or disclose.

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download, updates and any statistics or crash reports it provides to developers under its own privacy policy and your device settings. That is done by Google Play and Android, not by the app.

## Children

The app is a general-audience sports tool and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, if a future version needs internet access), this page will be updated and the effective date below changed before that version is released.
